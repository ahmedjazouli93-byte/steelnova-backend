# Steelnova Solutions - Knowledge Base

## About Steelnova Solutions
- **Location:** Zone Industrielle Sidi Bernoussi, Casablanca, Morocco.
- **Activity:** Wholesale distribution and supply of raw metallurgical products, structural steel profiles, and precision laser cutting services.

## Product Portfolio & Offerings
- **Structural Steel Profiles:** HEA, HEB, IPE, UPN, IPN beams, equal/unequal angles, square/rectangular hollow sections.
- **Tubes & Piping:** Seamless steel tubes, welded industrial tubes, galvanized pipes.
- **Sheets & Plates:** Cold-rolled sheets, hot-rolled steel plates, galvanized sheets, stainless steel, aluminum plates.
- **Services:** Custom industrial laser cutting, sheet metal shearing, bending, and custom cutting to spec.

## Inquiries & Ordering
- Deliveries dispatched directly from Casablanca across Morocco.
- Custom quotes provided based on dimensions, thickness, and volume.
